Open web appNative downloadsInstallation guide ↗

iOS builds require a compatible signing or alternative-store installation method. Hardware and background features vary by platform. The web app can be installed from supported browsers.

A little clarity about privacy.

Ride records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Peer features use a signaling service to establish connections; routing, maps and address searches can contact their providers. Location, Bluetooth, microphone and camera features need the corresponding permissions. Review Data privacy in the app for details.

Velunivo is open source under AGPL-3.0-only. Check road signs and local rules: a suggested route does not establish permission to ride.
