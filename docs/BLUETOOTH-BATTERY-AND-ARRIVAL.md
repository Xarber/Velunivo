# Bluetooth battery learning and automatic arrival

Real Bluetooth rides never request manual battery usage, including old pending rides and collection-disabled sessions. Battery calculation uses stored start/end readings rather than requiring the radio to still be connected at completion. Simulations remain excluded. Missing endpoints, charging/increasing charge, long battery gaps and stale endpoints do not produce fabricated consumption or training data.

Completed rides are migrated lazily on archive load, once per analysis/geometry version. Original GPS fixes, compass, motion and vehicle readings remain intact. Derived paths receive the existing accuracy-aware stationary deadzone and gentle short-window straightening; real detours and interruptions remain. Distance/preview are recalculated from usable geometry. Only already-arrived records have an overlong end timestamp reduced to the final stored fix; historical arrival at an unknown destination is not guessed.

Each actual battery decrease also retains a bounded interval with distance, measured speed, mode (only when consistent), voltage and available ascent/descent, allowing battery use to be compared with those conditions. Energy summaries retain measured moving speed (vehicle speed with GPS fallback), start/end/mean voltage, time in each reported riding mode, battery percent per kilometre and explicitly reported electrical power integrated over valid short intervals, with coverage seconds. Current sign does not imply consumption/charging. Ascent/descent use actual GPS elevation only when reported vertical accuracy is acceptable; older rides without elevation stay unknown. New foreground/background fixes preserve Expo Location's optional altitude/altitudeAccuracy fields: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/location/ .

Range learning uses real recorded speed as its reference. With at least three comparable reports it prefers the active riding mode and similar voltage; known climb plus configured battery capacity/mass remove the modeled uphill component to avoid adding it twice. This is a transparent range model, not a fitted manufacturer battery model. Voltage is a comparison context, never converted blindly to charge percentage.

Vehicle full-charge range is persisted automatically only after at least ten real Bluetooth battery rides, each at least 500 m and 2 percentage points consumed, at least 50 total percentage points measured, and a consumption-rate coefficient of variation no greater than 25%. Shared public scooters are excluded. Persistent range remains in kilometres internally regardless of presentation units.

Navigation now enters an explicit arrived state at the existing precise endpoint/progress check. GPS/simulation updates halt immediately, the final ride is saved, background location/directions stop, and the app shows a completion screen. Motion, screen wake lock, sharing and Live Activities are released. Dismissing completion resets Explore to the initial Your ride screen (Plan a ride / Record trip). Manually ending navigation at or beyond 50% progress also resets that screen; earlier manual stops keep the preview. Manual trip recording without a destination retains manual stop behavior.

Reverse-coordinate lookup exposes a shared pending state: a small indicator appears to the left of the coordinate text and the text becomes muted until completion, failure or timeout. Saved-place names resolve immediately without network loading. Sharing scooter identity pages use “Speed Limits”; other vehicles keep “Name & speed limits”.

Validation: automated migration, battery provenance/freshness, threshold, mode/speed/voltage, hill-data absence, stationary-path and coordinate-activity tests; full lint/typecheck suite. A headless Chrome GPS-mode ride reached the arrival screen, rejected subsequent recording fixes, reset to initial Explore both after arrival and manual stopping beyond halfway, and showed left-side reverse-geocoding progress; a wake-lock activation/cleanup race discovered there was repaired with serialized leases. Physical Bluetooth/hill calibration is not claimed by these tests. No local native compilation.
